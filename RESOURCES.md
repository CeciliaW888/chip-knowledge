# Semiconductor Investing Resources

Curated, high-trust sources. Knowledge in lessons is drawn from here, not from memory.

## Knowledge

- ★ **OWNED — Book:《芯片战争》余盛 (2021)** — `reference/芯片战争 (余盛) (Z-Library).epub` · chapter map in [book-index-chip-war-yusheng.md](./book-index-chip-war-yusheng.md)
  Chinese-language industry history. The course's anchor reference for *how the industry got its shape*. Uniquely deep on ASML's origin (ch.5–6), the foundry wars (ch.9), memory (ch.4,8,10), EUV (ch.19), ARM/AMD/Nvidia (ch.21), and the entire **China** story (ch.12–20). Use for: historical grounding of every moat — how TSMC/ASML/Samsung actually won. Caveat: 2021, Chinese vantage point — cross-check live figures against SemiAnalysis/SIA.
- [Book: _Chip War_ — Chris Miller](https://www.christophermiller.net/semiconductors)
  The English-language counterpart (a *different* book, same translated title). Strong on US policy & geopolitics. Use for: a second perspective on the same history, in English. Optional — the owned book above covers the same ground more deeply.
- [Asianometry — YouTube channel (Jon Y)](https://www.youtube.com/@Asianometry)
  Clear, visual, deeply researched explainers on how chips are made and the history of each segment. Use for: understanding the technology behind any segment in plain language. Start with his "How to Make a Chip" / lithography videos.
- [Fabricated Knowledge — Doug O'Laughlin (Substack)](https://www.fabricatedknowledge.com/)
  Investor-grade semiconductor analysis; now merged into SemiAnalysis. Use for: business models, segment economics, cycle calls, single-company deep dives.
- [SemiAnalysis — Dylan Patel (Substack)](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/)
  The deepest open-web analysis on AI compute, fabs, and the equipment supply chain. Use for: AI-driven demand, leading-edge competitive dynamics, equipment chokepoints. Some content paywalled.
- [Semiconductor Industry Primer — Aaron Pek (valueinvesting.substack.com)](https://valueinvesting.substack.com/p/semiconductors)
  A from-scratch primer aimed at investors. Use for: a single-sitting overview of the whole value chain.
- [Quartr — Understanding the Semiconductor Value Chain](https://quartr.com/insights/company-research/understanding-the-semiconductor-value-chain-key-players-and-dynamics)
  Concise, well-structured value-chain map with the key players per segment. Use for: quick reference on who does what.
- [SIA — Semiconductor Industry Association (data & reports)](https://www.semiconductors.org/)
  Industry body; reliable market-size, capex, and policy data. Use for: hard numbers and trend data to sanity-check theses.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/SecurityAnalysis](https://www.reddit.com/r/SecurityAnalysis/)
  Serious, moderated investing discussion. Use for: critique of a moat thesis on a specific chip stock.
- [ChinaTalk (podcast/newsletter)](https://www.chinatalk.media/)
  Where Asianometry, Fabricated Knowledge, and SemiAnalysis frequently debate the industry together. Use for: hearing the best analysts pressure-test each other.
